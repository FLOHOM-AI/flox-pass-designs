Marina amenities and showers›

Restrooms and hot showers are in the marina building at the head of the pier; your gate card opens them. Trash and recycling bins are by the pier gate.

Fire pit how-to›
  1. Turn the valve under the table to ON.
  2. Press and hold the ignition button until the flame catches.
  3. Turn it fully off when you leave the deck, and by 10 PM for quiet hours.
Local tips: Fells Point, Federal Hill›

Fells Point: cobblestone streets, waterfront bars and the water taxi. Federal Hill: the park at the top has the best view of the harbor at sunset.

Parking›

Street and garage parking near 400 Key Highway, Baltimore, MD 21230. Your crew shares the best option for your dates in the check-in steps.
