Eleven ways the FLOx Pass could work

Each one is a working prototype of the same guest journey: verify ID, sign the house agreement, choose cover, crew review, then the pass with WiFi, check-in steps, key code, extras and messages. They only differ in how it looks and how you move through it. Nothing you enter is saved, uploaded or charged.

How to review (10 minutes)

  1. Open this page on your phone. Every prototype is built phone-first.
  2. Start with 00 The Film, then open two or three of the others that catch your eye.
  3. In each one, use the Demo controls button (top right) to skip ahead, as explained below.
  4. Tell Pray which one to build, or which parts to combine (for example "the film, then the Home Hub").

The Demo controls button, in every prototype

Guests never see it. It lets you play the guest and the crew at the same time.

Jump toGo straight to Verify, Review, Declined, the FLOx Pass, or After check-out.
Approve / DeclineAct as the crew reviewing the guest. Decline shows the "retake your photo" message.
Booking channelSwitch to Airbnb or VRBO: the ID photo step disappears, as in the real pass.
Stay lengthPick 1 night: only the refundable deposit is offered.
Pretend it's...Time travel. Pick "Fri 16 Oct, 1:30 PM" and WiFi, check-in steps and the key code unlock.
Fill sample answersSkips typing: fills ID, signature and cover so it submits for review.

To try the ID step for real, tap Add and take any photo. It stays on your phone.
